# Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge
We pledge to make participation in EnterpriseRAG a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, gender identity, experience level, nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual orientation.

## Standards
- Use welcoming and inclusive language.
- Be respectful of differing viewpoints.
- Focus on what is best for the community and product quality.
