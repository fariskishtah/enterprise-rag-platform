🚀 Excited to launch **EnterpriseRAG**—a local-first multimodal knowledge intelligence platform designed for zero-hallucination AI research!

Key highlights:
- 📄 **Multimodal RAG**: PDF, DOCX, scanned PDFs (OCR), tables, audio & video.
- 🎬 **Video Intelligence**: Interactive Whisper transcript timestamps sync with video playback.
- 🌍 **Multilingual**: Cross-lingual Arabic & English QA with dynamic RTL interface.
- ⚡ **8 GB Memory Optimised**: Reduced multi-prompt Compare from 6 LLM calls to 1 single call (>80% latency reduction) with process concurrency queueing.
- 📊 **Evaluation & Feedback**: Integrated Evaluation Dashboard and answer feedback pipelines.
- 🐳 **Docker & HF Spaces**: Deployable as a single-container Hugging Face Docker Space.

Built 100% open-source with Python, FastAPI, React, TypeScript, Qwen2.5, LangChain, FAISS, and Whisper.
